About this tool

How Squeeze works

Private

Your file stays put

Compression runs in your browser. Your PDF is never sent to our servers or anyone else's.

Keep text

Smarter compression

Large photos and scans inside the PDF are re-compressed, while text, links and forms are left alone.

Smallest size

Maximum squeeze

Every page becomes a compressed image. It gives the smallest files, but text can no longer be selected.

Good to know

  • PDFs that are mostly text are already small, so there may be little or nothing to save.
  • Password-protected PDFs aren't supported. Remove the password first.
  • Changing a PDF breaks any digital signature it carries.
  • Very large files use a lot of memory. On a phone, try the Light level or a computer.
  • Always keep your original file. Squeeze never changes it.
